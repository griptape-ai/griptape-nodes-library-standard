- `Rotate Image`, `Flip Image`, `Grayscale Image`, `Adjust Image Levels`, `Adjust Image EQ`,
  `Gaussian Blur Image`, `Bloom Effect` and `Resize Image` show a preview of their result above their
  settings, which follows the settings while you drag them. The node still processes the image when
  it runs. Requires an editor and engine that support widget `synced_parameters`.
