- `Trim Audio` trims audio to a start and end time. Its waveform player has draggable in and out
  markers that set `start_time` and `end_time`, plays while you scrub, and has Mark In and Mark Out
  buttons (also the I and O keys) that set a point at the current time. You can also type the times
  or wire them in. An `end_time` of 0 keeps everything after the start.
  https://github.com/griptape-ai/griptape-nodes-library-standard/issues/643
