- `Adjust Video EQ`, `Flip Video`, `Blur Video`, `Add Vignette`, `Add RGB Shift`, `Add Color Curves`
  and `Add Film Grain` show a preview of their effect on a frame of the video, with a scrubber to pick
  the frame. It follows the settings while you drag them. `Add Film Grain`'s grain and the blurs are
  close approximations. Requires an editor and engine that support widget `synced_parameters`.
