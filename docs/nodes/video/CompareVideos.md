---
description: Shows two videos together so you can compare them.
when_to_use: Use it when you want to check two versions of a clip against each other, such as before and after an effect.
---

## When to use it

You have two videos and want to judge the difference, for example the original and a graded or upscaled version. Connect one video to `Video_1` and the other to `Video_2`. The comparison appears on the node.

The node does not process or change either video.

## Parameters

### Compare

Holds the two connected videos as `input_video_1` and `input_video_2`. It updates as soon as either input changes, without running the workflow. It is a display control, so you don't edit it directly.
