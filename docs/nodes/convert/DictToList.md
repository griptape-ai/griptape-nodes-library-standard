---
description: Splits a dictionary into a list of its keys and a list of its values.
when_to_use: Use it when you need to loop over the names or the contents of a dictionary.
---

## When to use it

Connect a dictionary and get two lists in matching order. `keys` holds the names and `values` holds what is stored under them. If the input isn't a dictionary, both lists are empty.

Both outputs appear once a dictionary is set.
